YolUp ("ilova", "biz") — o'quv markazlari, o'qituvchilar, o'quvchilar va ota-onalar uchun onlayn ta'lim xizmati. Ushbu siyosat YolUp mobil ilovasi va unga bog'liq xizmatlarda qaysi ma'lumotlar to'planishi, nima uchun ishlatilishi va qanday himoyalanishini tushuntiradi.

**Ma'lumotlar uchun mas'ul:** [hisob egasining to'liq ismi yoki tashkilot nomi], [manzil], e-pochta: [aloqa e-pochtasi].

### 1. Qanday ma'lumotlar to'planadi

**Hisob ma'lumotlari**
- **Majburiy:** ism, familiya, login, parol (shifrlangan holda saqlanadi), rol (o'qituvchi, o'quvchi, ota-ona).
- **Ixtiyoriy:** telefon raqami, e-pochta, tug'ilgan sana, shahar, sinf, fan, o'zingiz haqingizda qisqa ma'lumot, profil rasmi.

**O'quv faoliyati**
- Guruhlar va darslar.
- Uy vazifalari va ularga yuborilgan javoblar (matn, rasm, fayl, ovoz).
- Test va imtihon natijalari, baholar.
- O'qituvchi sertifikatlari.

**Muloqot**
- Guruh va shaxsiy chatlardagi xabarlar, fayllar, ovozli xabarlar.

**Jonli darslar**
- Kamera tasviri va ovoz — faqat siz jonli darsga kirib, ularni yoqqaningizda.
- O'qituvchi yoqqan bo'lsa, dars yozib olinishi mumkin.
- Ekran ulashish — faqat siz uni ishga tushirganingizda.

**Davomat va diqqat**
- Darsga kirish va chiqish vaqti, darsda bo'lgan muddat.
- Dars paytida ilova ochiq turganmi (diqqat tahlili) va diqqat tekshiruvlariga javoblar.

**Xavfsizlik ma'lumotlari**
- Hisobga kirishlar tarixi: sana, IP manzil, IP bo'yicha aniqlangan taxminiy joylashuv (shahar va mamlakat), qurilma va operatsion tizim nomi.

**Bildirishnomalar**
- Push-bildirishnomalar yuborish uchun qurilma identifikatori (token).

Biz **to'plamaymiz**: aniq GPS joylashuvi, kontaktlar, kalendar, to'lov ma'lumotlari. Ilovada reklama va uchinchi tomon analitika vositalari yo'q.

### 2. Ma'lumotlar nima uchun ishlatiladi

- Xizmatni ko'rsatish uchun: darslar, vazifalar, testlar, chat, natijalar.
- Hisobni yuritish uchun: kirish, parolni tiklash, ikki bosqichli tasdiq.
- O'qituvchi va ota-onaga o'quvchining davomati, vazifalari va natijalarini ko'rsatish uchun.
- Xavfsizlik uchun: notanish qurilmadan kirish haqida ogohlantirish va firibgarlikning oldini olish.
- Darslar, vazifalar va xabarlar haqida bildirishnomalar yuborish uchun.

### 3. Sun'iy intellekt

Vazifalarni tezkor tekshirish va AI repetitor funksiyalari uchun vazifa matni va javoblar sun'iy intellekt xizmati ko'rsatuvchisiga yuboriladi. Ular faqat shu so'rovga javob olish uchun ishlatiladi. [AI xizmati ko'rsatuvchisi nomi — tekshirilsin.]

### 4. Ma'lumotlarni kim ko'radi

- **O'qituvchi:** o'z guruhidagi o'quvchilarning vazifalari, natijalari, davomati va xabarlari.
- **Ota-ona:** faqat o'ziga bog'langan farzandining davomati, vazifalari, natijalari va kirishlar tarixi. Bog'lanish uchun farzandning roziligi so'raladi. Farzand bog'lanishni istalgan vaqtda uzishi mumkin.
- **Ota-ona ruxsatlari:** dars yozuvi, kamera va faollik tahlilini ota-ona o'zi yoqadi yoki o'chiradi.
- **Xizmat ko'rsatuvchilar:** server joylashuvi, video aloqa, push-bildirishnomalar (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) va sun'iy intellekt — faqat bizning topshirig'imiz bilan va xizmatni ko'rsatish uchun.

Biz shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni sotmaymiz va reklama uchun uzatmaymiz.

### 5. Saqlash va himoya

- Ilova va server orasidagi barcha ma'lumotlar shifrlangan aloqa (HTTPS/WSS) orqali uzatiladi.
- Parollar ochiq holda saqlanmaydi.

- Hisob o'chirilgach, ma'lumotlar [muddat] ichida o'chiriladi. Qonun talab qiladigan ma'lumotlar bundan mustasno.

### 6. Sizning huquqlaringiz

- Profil ma'lumotlarini ilovada ko'rish va o'zgartirish.
- Ilovada hisobni o'chirish: Profil → Akkaunt sozlamalari (ota-onada: Profil → Xavfsizlik) → "Akkauntni o'chirish".
- Ilovaga kira olmasangiz, [aloqa e-pochtasi] ga yozib, hisobni o'chirishni so'rash.

## English

**Effective date:** [date]

YolUp ("the app", "we") is an online learning service for learning centres, teachers, students and parents. This policy explains what data the YolUp mobile app and related services collect, why, and how it is protected.

**Data controller:** [account owner's full name or organisation], [address], email: [contact email].

### 1. Data we collect

**Account data**
- **Required:** first and last name, username, password (stored hashed), role (teacher, student or parent).
- **Optional:** phone number, email, date of birth, city, grade, subject, short bio, profile photo.

**Learning activity**
- Groups and lessons.
- Homework and submitted answers (text, photo, file, voice).
- Test and exam results, grades.
- Teacher certificates.

**Communication**
- Messages, files and voice messages in group and private chats.

**Live lessons**
- Camera and microphone, only when you join a live lesson and turn them on.
- Lessons may be recorded when the teacher enables recording.
- Screen sharing, only when you start it.

**Attendance and focus**
- Join and leave times and time spent in a lesson.
- Whether the app stayed open during the lesson (focus analytics) and answers to attention checks.

**Security data**
- Login history: date, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP (city and country), device and OS name.

**Notifications**
- A device token used to send push notifications.

We do **not** collect precise GPS location, contacts, calendar or payment data. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics.

### 2. How we use data

- To provide the service: lessons, homework, tests, chat, results.
- To manage your account: sign-in, password recovery, two-factor verification.
- To show teachers and parents a student's attendance, homework and results.
- For security: alerts about sign-ins from new devices and fraud prevention.
- To send notifications about lessons, homework and messages.

### 3. Artificial intelligence

For fast homework checking and the AI tutor, the task text and answers are sent to an AI service provider and used only to produce that response. [Name of the AI provider — to be confirmed.]

### 4. Who can see your data

- **Teachers:** homework, results, attendance and messages of students in their groups.
- **Parents:** only the attendance, homework, results and login history of children linked to them. Linking requires the child's consent, and the child can unlink at any time.
- **Parental permissions:** the parent turns lesson recording, camera and activity analytics on or off.
- **Service providers:** hosting, video communication, push notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging) and AI, acting on our behalf only to provide the service.

We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

### 5. Storage and security

- All data between the app and our servers is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS).
- Passwords are never stored in plain text.
- Data is kept while the account is active.
- After an account is deleted, its data is erased within [period], except where the law requires us to keep it.

### 6. Your rights

- View and edit your profile in the app.
- Delete your account in the app: Profile → Account settings (parents: Profile → Security) → "Delete account".
- If you cannot sign in, ask for deletion by writing to [contact email].
- Revoke camera, microphone or notification permissions at any time in your phone settings.

### 7. Children

YolUp is used through learning centres. A parent may create or link a minor student's account. The parent then sees the child's data and controls the permissions. [Additional clause on children under 13 — depends on the target-audience decision.]
